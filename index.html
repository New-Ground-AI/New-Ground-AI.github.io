03 / The community

People

Meet the researchers ↗ Join us ↗

New Ground AI is a research collective spanning universities, labs, and industry. Members work at or have trained at Stanford University, McGill University, Mila, MBZUAI, MIT, the University of Cambridge, Imperial College London, City University of Hong Kong, and more, as well as at places such as Salesforce, RBC Borealis, Wayfair, and Next Pathway.

The group includes undergraduates, graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and engineers. Together they study generative models, agentic systems, retrieval, alignment, and multimodal intelligence, and carry that work into production systems.

Meet our researchers, and Welcome to join us, to build advanced, analytical and adaptive intelligence together.
