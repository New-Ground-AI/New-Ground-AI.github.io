Weien (Barry) Li received his B.Sc. in Computer Science from McGill University in 2023. He also received a Master of Business Analytics from MIT in 2025.

He works on multimodal retrieval, agent evaluation, and a Secret Area :D. During his studies he held several research assistantships at both MIT and McGill. Barry was a Forward Deployed Engineer and a Data Scientist at Invisible Technologies, and he is a Machine Learning Scientist at Wayfair.
